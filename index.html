Starter checklist

Five actions for this week

  1. Review one job advertisement for gendered or exclusionary wording.
  2. Check that promotion and performance criteria are written and evidence-based.
  3. Discuss whether salary ranges and pay-review processes are transparent.
  4. Confirm how employees can report concerns safely and confidentially.
  5. Credit ideas fairly and invite overlooked voices into decisions.

    About WeLeadEqual

    An expert-informed learning tool supporting SDG 5.5.2.

    WeLeadEqual is a student project combining an Instagram awareness campaign with a web-based reflection tool focused on fair participation in workplace management and leadership.

    01

    Project purpose

    Help students, early-career professionals and campaign viewers recognise everyday workplace bias and consider fairer alternatives.

    02

    Expert-informed design

    Scenarios reflect interview insights on motherhood stigma, perception double standards, discriminatory hiring questions, competency and transparency.

    03

    Privacy boundary

    The assessment runs entirely in the browser. It does not request or transmit names, contact details, employer information or answers.

    04

    Educational boundary

    This tool is not a diagnostic test, legal service, complaint channel or substitute for an organisation’s formal policies and support systems.

    Expert-informed design

    How interview insights changed the solution

    • Motherhood and family assumptions: reflected in recruitment, flexible-work and career-break scenarios.
    • Perception double standards: reflected in the “bossy versus decisive” performance-review scenario.
    • Competency instead of tokenism: reflected in evidence-based hiring, promotion and meaningful leadership participation.
    • Transparency and awareness: reflected in pay guidance, action tips, awareness conversations and resource links.
    • Data responsibility: addressed by keeping answers in the browser and clearly stating the tool’s privacy boundary.

    Privacy and educational notice

    No personal data: The assessment does not request names, emails, employer details or other identifying information.
